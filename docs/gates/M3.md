## Gate scenario (`tests/e2e/m3.gate.spec.ts`)

Five pages take part: Anna, Ben, Cleo and Dan play, and Viv watches as a spectator. They use `?transport=local`, `?profile=a`–`e`, fixture card data, `?faces=placeholder` and `?speech=fake`. The scenario:
- **Two-Headed Giant lobby.** Teams by chair; the spectator joins by the watch link.
- **Hands.** Every page shows its own seat at the bottom. Each teammate's page shows the partner's hand as a second fan.
- **Voice.** All four players join voice. The talk indicator goes through no mic, idle, live, team (only the teammate's track opens), muted, turn mic and dictating.
- **Play.**
  - A three-way stack: a creature spell, a held Essence Scatter with a stack-to-stack target line, and a held Shock at an avatar. It is resolved and countered.
  - Attack lines to both opponents.
  - A team reveal to the opposing team.
  - Team life changes.
- **Captions.** A fake-engine caption appears on the attacking card on every page, the spectator's included, and is stored on its action in the log. The prompt's OK and Hold buttons are checked to be the topmost element (`elementFromPoint`) on each answering page.
- **Free-for-all.** Three-player and four-player openings.
- **Results.**
  - All pages converge on one state hash.
  - The leak scan is clean: team hands reach only the teammate's connection, no opponent receives the other team's hidden cards, and the spectator receives none.
  - No step falls back to the debug hook.

## Fixes after the workflow

- **Blocking (gate screenshots).** At a Two-Headed Giant table, the teammate's hand fan covered the OK/Hold prompt. The tests had missed it because they check visibility, not occlusion. Now the prompt, toast and untap offer sit just above the teammate's fan, at z-index 20, and the gate asserts that the prompt buttons are on top.
- **Blocking (M3-P4 review), slow transcriptions lost.** A slow on-device transcription could lose its final, or store a cut-off one, because the 3 s grace was counted from the key release. Now a caption run stays open until the engine reports that nothing is owed, with a 60 s cap.
- **Blocking (M3-P4 review), note dictation leaked.** Words dictated into a note field could become a public caption and log entry: when the field closed while the key was held, when it lost focus, or when it was removed without focusout. Now each run has a fixed destination, a note run never produces a caption, and orphaned note words are dropped. Ten new unit tests; 9 failed before the fix.
- **Minor:**
  - The caption log line now names its action ("Anna draws an attack line: '…'").
  - The voice panel overlays the avatar column instead of crowding the own avatar.
  - The on-device model's download confirmation names jsDelivr and Hugging Face.
  - Interim presence is no longer overwritten by an older press, and a late final goes only to the note that was open when dictation started.
- **Fixed by the gate fix round:**
  - the start-page nickname race, which had made CI red on WebKit;
  - the avatar column overflow at four seats (compact avatars);
  - the teammate fan kept clear of the table fit;
  - four-player side-seat pile labels;
  - log wording;
  - a read-only spectator HUD.

## Open questions for the owner

- **Three-player layout from seats 2 and 3.** The design's format table says "you at the bottom, opponents at top-left and top-right". The geometry spec (seat angles 0/180/180) instead puts one opponent beside you on your edge when seen from seat 2 or 3. The code follows the geometry spec (`[M3-P1]` decision).
- **Spectators hearing the table.** Players send their audio to spectators, but a spectator's page does not play it yet: it has no voice session. The design is silent on this. Either remove the outgoing track to spectators, or give spectators a listen-only voice session.
- **Talk key in note fields.** Holding V while a note field has focus types a "v", so the keyboard cannot start note dictation; only the talk mouse button can. A non-character talk key or a hold threshold would fix it.
- **Two-Headed Giant without the lobby.** A room created directly as Two-Headed Giant has no teams until the format is chosen in the lobby (`[M3-P1]` follow-up).
- **On-device speech engine.** It loads transformers.js and the ONNX runtime from jsDelivr, without integrity checks, which a dynamic import cannot have. The alternative is bundling them as a lazily loaded chunk. Model size, accuracy and Firefox capture are not yet confirmed on real hardware.
- **Web Speech in Chrome.** If Chrome aborts the previous recognition when a new one starts, a very quick re-press can lose that press's final. This was not verified in a real browser.

## Owner playtest checklist

1. **Two-Headed Giant over the live URL** with real microphones.
   - Choose Two-Headed Giant in the lobby and form teams by chair.
   - Check the teammate's hand fan, shared life, team chat and a team reveal.
   - Hold priority with H during a stack, and check that the prompt stays readable.
2. **Voice.**
   - Try push-to-talk (V), team talk (Shift+V), the turn mic, open mic, and mute (M).
   - Check the speaking rings, per-player volume and the device picker.
3. **Dictation.**
   - Try Web Speech captions on your own turn: the caption should sit on the last card you touched, and the log should show it with its action.
   - Try dictating into a note.
   - Optionally choose the on-device engine and judge the download prompt and the accuracy.
4. **Three- and four-player tables.** View them from your seat and decide the three-player question above.
5. **Spectator.** Open the watch link in another browser and check what the spectator sees.
