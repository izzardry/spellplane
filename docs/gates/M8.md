## Decisions made beyond the design

`docs/DECISIONS.md` holds 357 `[M8…]` lines (plan, contracts, packages, integration) and 94 `[M7-P…]` lines. The ones the owner should look at first:

- **Plain is the default look.** The four M4 themes stay loadable but are out of the picker, and their gates switch them through the debug hook.
- **Panels.** The kit split `--ds-radius-md` into button and panel radii. Older components still use `md` for panels, which is F1.
- **The cover** is a gradient, not the blurred-out arena still. The still exists behind Home.
- **Sparring.** A solo game left after fewer than ten own actions is purged, not kept (contracts.md Part G).
- **Leaving** never asks: the arrow saves, and the toast offers Continue.
- **The rejoin rule.** Rejoin compares (moves, sequence number, peer) and the copy with more moves wins. The gate proves it with Ann's copy holding more sequence numbers.
- **Inviting a player back** needs 30 s of absence on the sequencer's clock. A new sequencer restarts the 30 s.

## What to look at on the preview link (in the order of the journey)

1. **Home.** Open https://izzardry.github.io/spellplane/preview/ in a fresh window: Home, not a table. There is one primary (`PLAY`), four mode cards, the soft arena behind, and no log.
2. **A first `PLAY`.** `SOLO · SPARRING` → `PLAY`: a table with seven cards, the log closed, Sparring answering. Notice the Welcome hint over Sparring's side (F2). Leave by the arrow top left: Home shows the `CONTINUE` strip.
3. **Decks.**
   - **A custom deck:** `DECKS` → `NEW DECK` → `CUSTOM DECK`. Search, click cards, then the lands helper; the curve and the strength follow.
   - **Six boosters:** `FROM BOOSTERS` → `OPEN BOOSTERS` opens six packs into a 40-card booster deck with a lock. Search is gone: the pool replaces it.
   - **Convert:** `CONVERT TO CUSTOM DECK` makes "<name> (custom)", which takes any card. The original stays sealed.
4. **A private game in two tabs.**
   - Tab A: `…/spellplane/preview/?transport=local&profile=a` → `PRIVATE GAME` → `CREATE TABLE` → `COPY LINK`.
   - Tab B: paste the link with `?transport=local&profile=b` in its query.
   - Both `READY`: the start counts itself down, then Versus, then seven cards each.
   - Look at the waiting room's settings column (F5).
5. **Quick match in two tabs.** Profiles a and b (each with a deck), `QUICK MATCH` → `FIND MATCH` in both. Browse Decks in one while the queue bar runs. Match found → `ACCEPT` in both → the table, with no waiting room.
6. **Leave and History.** Leave a private game by the arrow; the other tab sees you away. `HISTORY` lists it first; open it to be back on the same table.
7. **Bring … back.** With one tab gone for more than 30 s, open its plate's `⋯`: `BRING … BACK` shows the QR code and the link. Open the link in a third profile (`profile=c`): the claim page, `SIT DOWN HERE AS …`, then the recovery panel. Look at the claim and Gathering cards in Plain (F1).
8. **The four looks.** At a table, Menu → `Look…`: Plain, Kitchen Table, Graphite, Codex. Tilt with a right-drag on the empty table, from first person to bird's-eye.
9. **Safari against Chrome.** The same table in both, with `?fps` for the meter.
