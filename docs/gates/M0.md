## Gate scenario (`tests/e2e/m0.gate.spec.ts`)

Every step goes through the real UI: there is no `__spellplane.dispatch` fallback. The steps:
1. Paste `gw-m19-60.txt` into the deck import dialog.
2. S to shuffle, then draw seven.
3. Mulligan with Ctrl+M, then bottom one card with Shift+L.
4. Search the library for Leonin Warleader, and Mill 3.
5. Draw with D until a land is in hand.
6. Drag the land from the hand fan onto the battlefield, then tap it with T.
7. Cast with C and resolve with R.
8. Press + twice to add two counters.
9. Ctrl+T, choosing the related Cat token.
10. Attack with A, then settle with B.
11. G, then E.
12. Undo with Ctrl+Z.
13. Reload, and check that the state hash is unchanged.

## Screenshot review

The first gate run found one blocking defect: a resolved permanent landed at the table centre, inside the stack lane. It also found several minor ones. The fix round (`6666f28`) made these changes:
- **Resolve placement.** Resolve now uses the game's default destination and a free slot in the controller's territory.
- **Attacking cards.** They now show a ghost outline at the home slot and a chevron displacement marker. This was originally M1-P4 scope; it was built now because the theme tokens already existed.
- **Zone labels.** They are now drawn under the cards.
- **Counter badges.** They sit over the art and stay legible.
- **Camera fit.** The fit now leaves room for the HUD (log, hand), so your own piles are not covered.
- **Log.** Names are frozen at the time of each line, repeated actions are grouped into one line, and a shuffle reads as "shuffles".
- **Empty chair.** In solo, the empty second seat has no avatar.

The main session checked `mid-game-board` and `attack` itself; they match the review.

Minor findings still open (none blocks the gate):
- At the default fit, zone and pile labels are about 3 px tall on a 1x display. The avatar buttons show the same counts.
- The local avatar is a fixed panel on the left of the screen, not centred outside the near table edge. This was chosen for the fit insets; confirm it in the playtest.
- Your own log shows "Hidden card" for the card you put on the bottom after a mulligan, although you know which card it is.
- A library search is logged as "looks at the top N cards".
- When the graveyard viewer is open, the pile on the table also fans out behind it.
- A tapped creature overlaps the token beside it by 1–3 mm; Tidy (Y) re-flows the cards.
- Open for M1-P2: instance ids stay stable when a card goes hand → library → graveyard, so a peer could link hidden history to a later reveal. The fix is to re-issue opaque ids when a card returns to a hidden zone.
- After a reload, log names are taken from the restored state, because the frozen-name memo lasts only for the page's lifetime.

## Decisions made beyond the design

See `docs/DECISIONS.md` under "## M0" (about 80 lines). These are the ones to review:
- **M0-P7 Solo session added to the plan.** No package owned the glue: the secret store, private effects, undo, persistence and `main.ts`.
- **Solo shuffle trace.** A solo shuffle is publicly traced as `reorder` until M1-P2 implements shuffleCommit/Done.
- **Face-down placement.** It exists as `SoloSession.playFaceDown`, but the HUD cannot reach it until M1-C adds it to the contract.
- **Undo** never reaches past a shuffle, mulligan or deck import, and the undo stack does not survive a reload.
- **Resolve.** R accepts the default destination; inline buttons on the stack chip offer the other destinations. There is no modal chooser.
- **HUD placement.** The HUD covers screen edges, and the scene's fit avoids them (`data-fit-insets`).

## Owner playtest checklist

1. Open the live URL in Chrome and in Safari. Paste a real 60-card decklist through Import deck, and check that the live Scryfall images load.
2. Goldfish a few turns:
   - draw, mulligan, play lands and tap them;
   - cast and resolve creatures;
   - add counters, create tokens, attack and settle;
   - use the graveyard and exile, and undo.
3. Judge the feel:
   - drag and magnetic snap, cascade versus squared piles;
   - the tap and flip animation;
   - zoom and pan inertia, Z to zoom on a card, inspect;
   - the hand fan.
4. Press `?`, remap a key and reload.
5. Close the tab, reopen it, and check that the table comes back unchanged.
6. Confirm or reject the left-hand avatar panel and the label sizes.
