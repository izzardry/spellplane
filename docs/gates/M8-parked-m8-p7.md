## Open review findings (blocking)

1. **Quick match cannot work on the production transport** (`src/net/lobby/room.ts:67`; matchmaking.md MM-1 and 2.2, 2.4 `Ticket.tid`). `LobbyRoom` built the broker with `tid: transport.selfId` in its constructor, before `join()`. `TrysteroTransport.selfId` is `''` until the join has loaded Trystero (`src/net/trystero.ts`), so in production the broker's tid stayed `''`. `seek()` signs with the real id, and the broker drops its own ticket (`ticket.tid !== s.tid`, `broker.ts:513`), so the status never leaves `watching`. Every test passed only because the Loopback and Broadcast transports know their id at construction. The reviewer demonstrated it with a scratch sim over a transport whose `selfId` is `''` until join.
   - Suggested fix: take the tid after the join, refuse `seek()` before it, and add a sim over such a transport.
   - **In `0834687`:** `join()` sets `state.tid` from `selfId` after the await and sets `ready`; `seek()` throws before `ready`. `tests/sim/lobby/harness.ts` adds a Trystero-shaped transport, and `broker.test.ts` has a test over it.
2. **The 30-second arrival rule is not enforced unless the table session already exists, and `moving` has no exit** (`src/session/matchmaker.ts:386`; contracts.md Part G "Matchmaking" item 2; PLAN M8-P7 Sim). The only timer lived in `runMatchedEntry` (`src/session/match-room.ts:149`), which starts only once `MatchedEntry` mounts with a chair. A non-creator waited without limit for seat 0's table. When the creator never arrived, or a member left the match room before arriving, the member stayed in `moving` for good: `cancel()`, `seek()` and non-table routes were ignored there, so the player could not search again until a reload. The acceptance was hollow on this point: `arrival.test.ts` ran the 30 s deadline itself and called `mm.stranded()`, and `matchmaker.test.ts` used a fake session that a real non-creator would not have.
   - Suggested fix: the matchmaker owns a 30 s arrival timer from `formed` on its own clock. `arrived(roomId)` cancels it. Otherwise it runs the stranded path itself: void the record, purge the summary, navigate off the room route and re-announce with the kept wait. A route away from the room or `cancel()` while `moving` abandons the match, and the property test drives the real timer.
   - **In `0834687`:** the matchmaker runs the deadline on its clock. A stranded member leaves the room route and resumes with its wait kept, and leaving the room or `CANCEL` while `moving` abandons the match. `arrival.test.ts` was rewritten. Three new `matchmaker.test.ts` cases (no table session at all, route away, `CANCEL` in `moving`) cover it.
3. **A hold from a peer outside the offer splits the match** (`src/net/lobby/broker.ts:300`; matchmaking.md 2.5 rule 5 and "Room derivation"; PLAN "all members agree on the member list", "a forged hold is ignored"). Early holds, which arrive before their offer, were stored per match id without a membership check, and `decide()` copied all of them into `hold.nonces`. `deriveRoom` hashes and seats every key of that map. Match ids are predictable, so any seeker with a valid ticket could send one early hold to one member: the members then derive different rooms, and one gets a non-member in its seat order. On that seat order `planOf` throws at `f.tickets[tid]!` (`matchmaker.ts:574`), and that broker stays in `moving`. The reviewer showed two members deriving `salt-heath-wind-64` with order [b8, a8] and `violet-slate-lilac-53` with order [a8, z8, b8]. The in-hold path filtered by membership; the early path did not. (The fourth finding in the pipeline output, cut off there, is this same defect described again.)
   - Suggested fix: keep only members' early holds in `decide()`, derive the room from the offer's members only, and add the scenario to the forged-hold sim.
   - **In `0834687`:** `decide()` keeps only members' early holds. `maybeFormed()` builds the nonce map from `offer.members` only, and it now returns the match to the queue (`memberOut(..., 'stale')`) when a member's ticket is gone instead of throwing. `broker.test.ts` adds "an early hold from a peer outside the offer never joins the room derivation".

`0834687` also carries smaller changes, described in its `[M8-P7]` DECISIONS lines:

- notices are taken once and cleared by a new search or `CANCEL`
- `MatchHost` shows a pending notice on mount
- Enter on a focused `DECLINE` declines
- `applyRatedResult` marks the record rated before the rating, and an unseated record player is not counted
