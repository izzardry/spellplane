## Fixes after the workflow

### Main session
- **Blocking (M4-P1 review): bird's-eye → ego lost the cursor point.**
  - **The defect.** From a panned or zoomed bird's-eye view, the cursor's table point did not stay put for 10–25% of cursors over the table. At 1.5× zoom it moved 300–600 px at worst. In 1v1 at 3× zoom it moved 153 px.
  - **The cause.** The ego limits (25°–60° pitch, the lean and dolly limits) cannot show every bird's-eye view.
  - **The fix.** When the limits cannot hold the point, the switch lands beyond them: pitch 5°–85°, then lean and dolly up to twice their limits. The camera still never goes below 120 mm or behind the seat. Later gestures never push it further out and never jump, and fit, seat keys, avatar taps, zoom-to-card and follow bring it back inside the limits.
  - **Tests.** New property tests run in both directions over every format and seat; they failed before the fix.
  - **The earlier finding.** The reviewer confirmed that round 3's ego → bird's-eye fix holds: 3,956 cases plus 1,500 property runs, all under 2 px.
- **Minor (M4-P1 review):** one zoom-out tick after an off-table bird's-eye switch moved the cursor point by up to 98 px. Fixed.
- **Blocking (CI): lit themes timed out under software GL.**
  - **The cause.** Lit themes redrew every animation frame. On CI's SwiftShader one frame cost 0.2–1.5 s, so the page had no time left between frames. Five trivial evaluates took 13–25 s, and a re-join took more than 15 s; that is the `signals.spec:812` "element not found".
  - **The fix** (`src/scene/pacing.ts`):
    - Ambient-only frames (lights, particles, shimmer, the pending pulse, the foil sheen) back off to four frame costs once a frame costs more than 50 ms. Changes still draw at once.
    - A held card no longer forces frames while nothing moves.
    - Face uploads grow with frame cost.
  - **Unchanged:** quality levels, CI budgets, timeouts and shards.
- **Blocking (Chromium): Dusk's line bloom was missing in the app.**
  - **The cause.** Bloom finds bright areas on a half-resolution copy. Dusk's 3 px antialiased line, under the 10° tilt with MSAA, fell between its sample rows.
  - **The fix.** The bloom copy of lines and ping rings is drawn at least 4 device px wide; the sharp line keeps its width. The glow is now about +40/255, with the spec's threshold unchanged at 8.
- **Real bug (net), found from the gate's "history diverged" log lines.**
  - **The bug.** On `pagehide`, the `?transport=local` transport reported every peer as gone to its own page. That page, still running, then saved "away" and "sequencer claim" records that nobody else received, and they forked on return. Trystero did the same after `beforeunload`.
  - **The fix.** Both transports now stay quiet to their own page while unloading. A fork repair that abandons only away and claim records no longer shows the warning.
  - **Tests.** `tests/sim/net/reload.test.ts` reproduces the fork for a sequencer reload, a sequencer close and reopen, and a follower reload. It fails before the fix.
- **Minor: Dusk outlines.** Selected is now saturated gold (`#ffbf40`, 2 mm) and hover a quiet warm grey, so the two differ clearly.
- **Gap: quality and follow had no controls** (`9b17d02`).
  - **The gap.** The stored quality was never applied to the scene, and the HUD had no control for quality or the follow toggles.
  - **The fix.** The HUD's theme popover now has a Quality select and the checkboxes "Follow the stack" and "Follow the active player". Changes are applied on change, on mount and across theme switches and reloads.
  - **Storage.** One quality value is stored, and each theme maps it to its own level. A separate value per theme would need a new contract key.
- **Harness: ego shots.** The matrix presses P at the centre of Ann's row, so the signalled cards are no longer under the log panel.

## Screenshot review

- **Who looked.** The gate verifier looked at every screenshot of the workflow's run through contact sheets and full-size crops. The main session checked:
  - the fitted view of all four themes in both rigs;
  - foil in all four themes;
  - the ego foil and attacking shots after the framing fix.
- **Mood.** Each theme matches DESIGN's theme table:
  - **Paper:** flat white with hairline zones.
  - **Dusk:** dark green felt with a wood rim, a warm lamp and a vignette; bird's-eye tilted 10°.
  - **Wyrm:** an obsidian scale relief with ember seams and drifting embers; ego by default.
  - **Stonetable:** a carved stone slab with a glowing rune border, a torch light and dust motes; ego by default.
- **Card faces.** Faces are never tinted or cropped; the lit pipeline measures them within 3% of the source. In the ego rig cards are seen in perspective, as the design's ego view intends.
- **Signals.**
  - Tapped, selected, hovered, displaced (dashed ghost and caret), attacking, blocking (block line in the seat colour) and face-down (each theme's own neutral back, never an official one) are distinguishable in every theme and rig.
  - Foil shows as a sheen rim and a diamond badge.
  - Each seat's lines are drawn in its seat colour, with its dash pattern.
  - Speaking shows as a ring in the seat colour, pending confirmation as an outline plus a "Waiting for …" tag, and away as a dimmed avatar with an AWAY tag.

Minor findings still open:
- **Lit line glow.** The glow fills the dash gaps, so in the lit themes two neighbouring seat colours rely mostly on hue. Wyrm's halo reads cold blue rather than fiery.
- **Line style per theme.** Stonetable lines have no rune ornaments, and Wyrm lines are not animated. The scene's line layer does not draw ribbons, ornaments or animation yet (`[M4-P3]`, `[M4-P5]` decisions).
- **Attack styling.** An attacking card in 1v1 looks like an Alt-moved, tapped card. This matches the design's definition of attack.
- **Ego pile column.** Ann's pile column is outside every ego shot. From the spec's ego seat (260 mm back, 620 mm up, 45° field of view, 35° pitch), your own near-right corner is out of view.

## Open questions for the owner

- **Out-of-range ego landings.** After a switch from a deep bird's-eye zoom, the ego view can land at an unusual pitch (11.7° in one example) to keep the cursor point fixed. Does it look acceptable in play?
- **Ego framing.** Your own pile column is out of view in ego, and pressing P with the pointer beside your cards spreads them toward the log panel; that is the design's rule that the point under the cursor stays put. Wyrm and Stonetable open in ego, so this is what players see first.
- **Solo actions after a reload.** A page that played alone after a real disconnect, and is then reloaded instead of reconnected, loses those solo actions when the room's history wins. The warning does show. A fix needs the page's own-action ownership saved across reloads (session or persisted save); candidate for M5-P6 hardening.
- **Texture headroom.** Ego texture memory is 366–382 MB against the 400 MB cap.

## Owner playtest checklist

1. **Each theme on the live URL**, with a real GPU.
   - Pick Paper, Dusk, Wyrm and Stonetable in turn from the theme picker.
   - Judge mood, legibility and frame rate with a full board.
   - Try High, Medium and Low in the theme popover's Quality select.
2. **Different themes per player.** Two players at one table use different themes. Switching theme mid-game should change nothing for the other player.
3. **Camera.**
   - Press P (or use View) to switch between bird's-eye and ego from different zooms and pointer positions.
   - Right-drag to change the pitch.
   - Try "Follow the stack" and "Follow the active player" (theme popover), seat keys 1–4 and avatar taps in both rigs.
   - With reduced motion on, the switch should be a cut.
4. **Signals in play.** Check hover, selection, foil (import a decklist with a `*F*` line), lines in seat colours, the pending confirmation and away in each theme.
5. **Sound.** Set Sound to "theme" or "on" in the theme picker and listen to each theme's room tone and cues.
6. **Reload mid-game.** No "history diverged" line should appear after an ordinary reload.
