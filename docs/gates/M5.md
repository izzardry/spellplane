## What M5 built

- **Touch (iPad Safari).**
  - **Gestures:** tap selects; drag moves; double-tap taps a card; long-press opens the radial menu with eight actions (tap/untap, flip, inspect, +1/+1, to hand, to graveyard, exile, to stack, plus a centre button for the full menu); long-press then drag draws a line, including from a collapsed stack item.
  - **Camera and seats:** one finger on empty table pans; two fingers pinch, pitch and fit; a tap on an avatar goes to that seat.
  - **Thumb bar and hand:** the thumb bar above the hand has Attack, Settle, Resolve, Ask and Talk. The hand fan scrolls and lifts a card for reading. Safari's callout, magnifier, double-tap zoom and pull-to-refresh are off.
- **Audit**, automatic at game end.
  - **When:** each browser publishes its shuffle nonces and orders once every chair has seen the game end; nothing is published mid-game.
  - **Checks:** every peer replays every shuffle, reorder and draw against the commitments.
  - **Verdicts:** shown per player in the log and an audit panel: verified (with "k of n draws checked" when some draws could not be checked), mismatch (which action and what disagreed), or neutral "could not be checked: records missing".
- **Offline and storage.**
  - **Offline:** a service worker precaches the app, so it loads and resumes a saved table offline, and an offline notice appears.
  - **Updates:** a new version waits and applies only between games ("New version available: it applies after this game").
  - **Images and storage:** an LRU image cache with a storage estimate, and a persistent-storage request.
  - **iPad install:** installable, with a manifest, own icons and a one-time Home Screen hint explaining Safari's 7-day wipe.
  - **Backup:** export and import of decks and saved tables to a file.
- **Accessibility.**
  - **Keyboard:** arrow keys move focus through the cards of a zone and between zones, with a visible focus ring. Focus never reaches another player's hand or your library.
  - **Screen readers:** the log is a polite live region.
  - **Colour:** seat glyphs appear beside seat colours, and Paper meets AA.
  - **Motion:** reduced motion cuts or fades every animation.
  - **axe:** no serious or critical issue on the lobby, table HUD (desktop and iPad), builder list and the M5 panels.
- **Finish.**
  - **Settings:** one settings panel (display and sound, voice, dictation, keys, card back, storage, About).
  - **Keys:** key remapping with conflict detection and reset; Shift+F10 is reserved.
  - **About and hints:** an About panel with the Fan Content Policy notice and the Scryfall and MTGJSON credits, and first-run hints that never block.
  - **Card back:** an opt-in official card back, loaded from backs.scryfall.io and never bundled.
  - **Empty and error states:** no deck, no cards found, no peers, relays unreachable, storage full, failed image.
  - **Two-Headed Giant:** teams formed by chair.
- **Hardening.**
  - **Protocol version 2:** a peer on another version sees a message naming both versions and what to do.
  - **Soak:** 5,000 actions on four simulated peers, with equal hashes and bounded engine memory.
  - **Undo** edge cases.
  - **Solo actions:** they survive a reload after a disconnect (see the limits below).
  - **Pass draft:** the driver ignores abandoned history.
- **Your table and voice decisions.**
  - **Three players:** opponents top-left and top-right from every seat.
  - **Spectators:** they hear the table's open voice, never team talk, and never send audio.
  - **Talk key:** Ctrl (either side) opens the mic only after 200 ms held alone, so chords stay shortcuts.
  - **Ego framing:** the default ego view shows your piles and battlefield at 1440×900 and on an iPad.
  - **Far-zoom stack:** below 72 px card height the stack folds into a count badge that opens on hover or tap.

## Fixes after the workflow (main session)

Four independent reviews, each reproduced before it counted:

- **Blocking (Audit): two ways to cheat past the audit.**
  - **Stacked library.** A modified client could stack its library with a "reorder" of the whole library. The audit accepted it, and the log even called it a shuffle.
  - **Unchecked draws.** It could also put a card into its library with an unplaced position, which stopped the draw check.
  - **The fix.** A library reorder that moves more than one card must stay within cards the player looked at since the last shuffle. Unplaced arrivals count as on top, as the honest client does. A reorder is never logged as a shuffle at a networked table.
  - **Tests.** New unit and simulation cases fail on the old code.
- **Minor (Audit).**
  - "✓ verified" no longer hides unchecked draws: the panel says "k of n draws checked" with a neutral mark.
  - The test-only tamper code is now compiled out of the production build (checked by grep).
- **Blocking (Hardening): solo actions lost after a reload.**
  - **The defect.** When a page's last save fell exactly where it was cut off, and the room played about 200 more actions, every solo action was dropped. Round 3 had brought this back.
  - **The fix.** Now every own action missing from the adopted history is proposed again, however old. The sequencer's memory of action ids answers any already applied.
  - **Tests.** A simulation runs several seeds, a follower and a sequencer page, with the room below and past 400 actions, plus a duplicate case.
- **Blocking (Accessibility): contrast and coverage.**
  - **Contrast.** The thumb bar's Resolve (dimmed most of the game) and an away player's avatar were below AA in Paper. Both now keep full-contrast text and mark the state with a dashed border.
  - **Coverage.** axe now also covers the iPad HUD with the radial menu, settings, audit panel, notices, confirm prompt, stack badge and an away avatar. That found one more real issue, the hand fan's missing role, which is fixed.
- **Minor (Accessibility).**
  - A press on the table ends keyboard focus.
  - Arrow keys stay inside open panels and the radial menu.
  - Shift+F10 can no longer be remapped.
  - Loading builder rows are no longer dimmed.
  - Stack badge rows show the seat glyph.
- **Minor (scene and touch).**
  - **Ghost card.** A touch drag end is now sent twice, so a lost message no longer leaves a ghost card on other screens.
  - **Ego camera.** It re-frames for HUD changes only while it is still at its default view, and not during a rig switch.
  - **Touch lines.** Long-press then drag from a collapsed stack item draws a line on the iPad.
  - **Start page.** It decides its one hint in a single step (a possible CI race).

## Known limits (accepted, recorded in DECISIONS)

- **Audit.**
  - A modified client can still move a single card to the top of its library unnoticed by the audit. The public action does not say which card moved; each move shows in everyone's log as "moves a card within their library".
  - A cheater can mark their own bundle incomplete and get the neutral "could not be checked" instead of a mismatch. This is the owner's rule for missing records.
  - A table closed mid-match is not audited.
  - While a seated player has not acknowledged a game's end, bundles wait without a time limit.
- **Solo actions after a reload.**
  - **Still lost:** actions already folded into a stored snapshot. That happens after more than 600 actions alone, or when the sequencer page saves in its first 6 s after a cut.
  - **Residual risk:** after several simultaneous failures, an action proposed again could apply twice. That happens only if the answering sequencer no longer remembers its id.
- **axe and the 3D table.** axe cannot judge text contrast over the WebGL canvas (it reports "needs review"), so HUD contrast over the table rests on the Paper unit checks.
- **Protocol 2 and the live M4 site.** The live M4 site (protocol 1) shows its older, generic "reload the page" message to a v2 peer; that cannot be changed from v2. After this merge, everyone needs to reload once.
