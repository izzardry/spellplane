## M0 Solo table

- [M0] Added package M0-P7 Solo session (`src/session/`, `main.ts`, `debug/`), second wave after M0-P1 and M0-P5 — the plan had no owner for the client that glues reducer, secret store, private effects, undo, persistence and app wiring. M0-P5's reload acceptance test moves to M0-P7.
- [M0] New lint element `session` (may import core, net, store, data, draft, games, voice, util; imported by ui and debug).
- [M0] Package agents may make additive-only changes to contract files (new optional fields or new exports) when unavoidable and must report them; they never change or remove existing contract members — keeps parallel packages unblocked.
- [M0] Module test pages: `?harness=<name>` in dev/test builds loads `src/<module>/harness.ts` instead of the app, so scene and HUD can be tested before the session exists.
- [M0-P1] Contract (additive): optional `ActionBase.restore` and exported `RestorePatch` in core/actions.ts — a single opposite action cannot bring back lossy effects (turn number, target lines removed with a resolved item, an identity dropped in a hidden zone, a pile re-laid around a new base); `inverse()` attaches the exact prior public records as a diff, the reducer applies it only on undoable types and refuses a patch that would break the invariants.
- [M0-P1] Poses are stored quantised to 0.01 mm / 0.01° with `rot` in [0, 360) — attachments follow their host by translation, and quantising makes a move and its undo land on identical doubles, so undo restores the exact hash.
- [M0-P1] Piles exist while a card references them; a one-card pile is valid (no auto-dissolve) — card actions name one card, so building a pile from two loose cards passes through a one-card pile. Members are laid out from the base: cascade 14 mm along the base card's own "toward the owner" axis, squared on the base pose. When the base leaves, the next card takes the old anchor; joining at index 0 with a pose moves the pile.
- [M0-P1] `move` within a zone: the base of a pile (pile field absent) drags the whole pile; any other member given a new pose pulls out singly; `pile: null` leaves explicitly. Moving to another zone leaves the pile and detaches both ways. A moved card takes the top of its zone's draw order unless `index` is given.
- [M0-P1] Attachments: both cards in one public zone; the k-th attachment is tucked at k × (10 right, 10 up) in the host's frame; attachments follow host translations (not rotations); zone draw order is unchanged (the scene draws attachments behind hosts); attachment lines are derived from `attachedTo`, not stored as Line records.
- [M0-P1] Entering any hidden zone resets a card to a canonical record (no identity, face down, untapped, no counters, note, pile, attachments or home, pose 0/0/0, controller = owner) and removes lines touching it; hidden zones take only their owner's cards and never tokens. Leaving a hidden zone for a public one: face up only with a public identity (or a token label), else face down.
- [M0-P1] `flip` down keeps the already public identity (`hide` drops it); `flip` up needs a public identity, `ref`+`salt`, or a token label. `reveal` on an identified card replaces the identity (verification is M1-P2's).
- [M0-P1] Card and player counters clamp at 0 and drop the key; life is not clamped.
- [M0-P1] Tokens: owner = controller = `controller` or the zone's seat; `copy` creates the token in the original's zone under its controller with identity, face index and label but no counters; `remove` removes tokens and table objects only, never from the stack, and clears marker sources, lines, attachments and pile places pointing at it.
- [M0-P1] Stack: every push goes on top (copy markers too); only the top item resolves or is countered; leaving removes lines touching the item and target lines drawn from its spell card. The reducer has no card data, so clients pass `to` from `GameDefinition.defaultDestination`; without it a resolving spell goes to its controller's battlefield, a countered one to its owner's graveyard. Moving a spell card off the stack removes its item; copies of a departed item lose `copyOf`.
- [M0-P1] `turnPass` raises the turn number, sets the active seat or team and clears every auto-pass, but keeps the phase — the reducer holds no phase list; the HUD dispatches `phaseSet` to the first phase in the same group.
- [M0-P1] The reducer checks structure, not roles (actor owns the hidden zone, spectators cannot move cards) — that is the sequencer's structural check (M1-P1).
- [M0-P1] `seq`: an applied action carrying `seq` stamps `state.seq`; a refused one returns the same object. Public records with no shared effect (ping, chat, caption, lookAt, revealTo, reorder, snapshotHash, sequencerClaim) change nothing but `seq`. `inverse()` returns null for an action that changed nothing.
- [M0-P1] `createTable` starts at stage 'lobby' and adds a public table zone 'stack' if the game definition lacks one; `seatLeave` clears name, peer and ready; `seatTake` refuses a seat held by another peer.
- [M0-P1] Not applied yet, refused with the owning package in the reason: ask/respond/autoPass (M1-P3), shuffleCommit/Nonce/Done and rngCommit/Reveal (M1-P2), draft actions (M2).
- [M0-P1] Contract (additive, amends M0-P1's own unmerged `RestorePatch`): the patch is compare-and-set — `RestoreStep {expect, put}` per field or record, new exports `RestoreStep` and `RestorableCardField` — the earlier whole-record patch let any undo rewrite any part of the table (zone visibility, seats, turn marker) and silently reverted other seats' later changes (DESIGN Undo: "There is no table-wide rewind"). It now reaches only fields `controller identity face faceIndex tapped pose home combat pile attachedTo counters note tokenLabel` of existing cards in public zones, the draw order of public zones, lines, the stack, the turn number and seat `autoPass`; every put value is validated like an action field; a step whose `expect` no longer matches refuses the undo ("undo is stale: …"); invariants are checked afterwards. A restore patch is accepted only on the inverse inside `undo`. An identity put back on a public card is structurally no stronger than `reveal`; checking it against the commitment is M1-P2's, as for reveals.
- [M0-P1] An undo that returns a card to a hidden zone drops its identity there, even if it was revealed in that zone before (DESIGN "Table model": peers drop the identity when a public card returns to a hidden zone); undo restores the previous hash except for that identity, which the owner can reveal again.
- [M0-P1] moveTemp/settle implemented in `core/reducer/temp.ts` (the M0 gate needs "attack and settle"; M1-P4 extends it): moveTemp only on a battlefield, sets `home` once, pulls a pile member out of its pile, sets the pose (attachments follow), sets `combat` when given, taps when `tap` is true and never untaps; settle (all, or `cards`) restores every displaced card to `home`, drops `home`/`combat`, removes attack and block lines (all, or those touching the listed cards), keeps marks and the tapped state. A permanent `move` with a pose, `tidy` or joining a pile forgets the home slot. Undo of settle is `settle {cards: []}` plus the restore patch.
- [M0-P1] A card entering a public zone that is not a battlefield (graveyard, exile, command, stack) loses tapped, counters, note and transformed face, as at a physical table; battlefield-to-battlefield moves keep them. Undo restores them.
- [M0-P1] `move` of a pile's base (pile field absent) from one battlefield to another takes the whole pile, re-laid at the new pose; any other cross-zone move of a base (e.g. to the graveyard) moves the base alone.
- [M0-P1] The reducer computes sine and cosine with its own range reduction and fixed Taylor polynomials in plain IEEE arithmetic, never `Math.sin`/`Math.cos`, whose last bit differs between engines (DESIGN "Data model and protocol": shared state identical in every browser).
- [M0-P1] Invariants also check hidden-zone cards are opaque records (face down, front face, pose 0/0/0, controller = owner, zone owned by the card's owner), card owners and controllers are seats, `combat` only with `home`, no displaced card in a pile, and the turn marker names a seat or team with a positive turn number.
